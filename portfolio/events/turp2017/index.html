Urban Resilience Tanzania (URTZ) 2017 Conference

Tanzania Urban Resilience Programme (TURP)

View All Images

Client: Understanding Risk Tanzania

Event: Introduction to the Tanzania Urban Resilience Programme

Venue: The Hyatt Regency Dar es Salaam, The Kilimanjaro

Project Details: Over the course of May 31st through June 2nd, 350 people gathered at The Kilimanjaro Hotel in Dar es Salaam to imagine the future of urbanization in Tanzania and surrounding regions. Participants included: 200+ government officials from TZ 16 honourable guests from surrounding nations 75 journalists from 42 African countries 10 local CSO representatives + donor partners and experts in the field of urban resilience The conference served as an introduction to the Tanzania Urban Resilience Programme – a partnership between the World Bank and the UK Department for International Development that aims to support the Government of Tanzania in its endeavour to improve the country’s resilience to climate and disaster risk.

Studio 19’s role: Event management, venue coordination, branding, setup and teardown, attendee registration, vendor management, and capturing still photos.
