CANSO Africa Conference 2019

CANSO Africa Conference

View All Images

Client: CANSO Africa Conference

Event: Week-long conference (2-6 September 2019)

Venue: Julius Nyerere International Conference Centre (JNICC), Dar es Salaam

Project Details: The conference brought together over 200 senior air traffic management delegates from the region and beyond.

Studio 19’s role: Event management, venue coordination, branding, setup and teardown, attendee registration, vendor management, organized three gala nights and entertainment, captured still photos, created a conference summary video, and organized an excursion to Zanzibar for the conference attendees.
